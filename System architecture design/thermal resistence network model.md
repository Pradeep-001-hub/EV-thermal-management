## Thermal Resistance Network Model

The complete Integrated Thermal Management System (iTMS) is modeled using a lumped-parameter thermal network. This model represents the thermal behavior of the battery pack, motor, inverter, HVAC system, coolant circuits, and heat exchangers using energy balance equations.

### Governing Energy Balance Equation

For each thermal node *i*:

\[
m_i C_{p,i}\frac{dT_i}{dt}
=
Q_{gen,i}
-
Q_{cool,i}
-
Q_{loss,i}
\]

### Variable Definitions

| Symbol | Description | Unit |
|----------|------------|------|
| \(m_i\) | Mass of thermal node *i* | kg |
| \(C_{p,i}\) | Specific heat capacity of node *i* | J/kg·K |
| \(T_i\) | Node temperature | K |
| \(Q_{gen,i}\) | Heat generation rate | W |
| \(Q_{cool,i}\) | Heat removed by cooling system | W |
| \(Q_{loss,i}\) | Heat loss to ambient environment | W |

---

### Coolant Heat Removal Model

The heat extracted by the coolant is calculated as:

\[
Q_{cool,i}
=
\dot{m}
C_{p,fluid}
(T_{out}-T_{in})
\]

### Variable Definitions

| Symbol | Description | Unit |
|----------|------------|------|
| \(\dot{m}\) | Coolant mass flow rate | kg/s |
| \(C_{p,fluid}\) | Specific heat capacity of coolant | J/kg·K |
| \(T_{out}\) | Coolant outlet temperature | K |
| \(T_{in}\) | Coolant inlet temperature | K |

---

### Physical Interpretation

The thermal model captures three fundamental thermal phenomena:

#### Heat Generation

Heat is generated by:

- Battery electrochemical losses
- Motor copper and iron losses
- Inverter switching losses
- DC-DC converter losses
- HVAC compressor operation

#### Heat Removal

Heat is removed through:

- Liquid cooling circuits
- Refrigerant cooling loops
- Heat exchangers
- Chillers
- Radiators
- Heat pumps

#### Environmental Heat Transfer

Heat exchange with the environment occurs through:

- Convection
- Conduction
- Radiation

and depends on:

- Ambient temperature
- Vehicle speed
- Solar loading
- Wind conditions
