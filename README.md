

## 📌 Project Title

**Integrated Thermal Management System for Electric Vehicles: A Holistic Approach Towards AI-Driven, Full-System Thermal Optimization for Next-Generation Mobility**

---

## 📄 Abstract

Electric Vehicles (EVs) represent a critical frontier in sustainable transportation; however, their widespread adoption continues to be constrained by thermal management challenges affecting battery packs, electric motors, power electronics, and cabin climate systems. Effective thermal regulation is essential not only for performance and efficiency but also for safety, battery longevity, charging capability, and passenger comfort.

This research presents a comprehensive investigation into an integrated Electric Vehicle Thermal Management System (EV-TMS) that unifies Battery Thermal Management Systems (BTMS), motor and power electronics cooling, and cabin HVAC operations within a single intelligent thermal architecture. Unlike conventional subsystem-specific approaches, the proposed framework emphasizes cross-system thermal integration and coordinated energy management.

The global EV thermal management market, valued at approximately **USD 27.96 billion in 2025**, is projected to reach **USD 78.67 billion by 2034**, growing at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately **16.1%**. This rapid expansion highlights the increasing demand for innovative thermal technologies capable of supporting next-generation electric mobility.

The proposed research investigates several advanced thermal management methodologies, including:

- Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) based predictive thermal control
- Immersion cooling using dielectric nanofluids
- Phase Change Material (PCM) enhanced thermal storage systems
- Silicon Carbide (SiC) power electronics thermal co-design
- Integrated and reconfigurable thermal circuit architectures
- Adaptive heat pump systems for energy-efficient cabin conditioning

Simulation, modeling, and validation are performed using:

- **Python** (TensorFlow, NumPy, SciPy)
- **MATLAB/Simulink**
- **Modern C/C++ Embedded Systems**

to ensure cross-platform reproducibility and future deployment feasibility.

The outcomes of this research aim to contribute toward the development of intelligent thermal management solutions for future electric vehicles while also providing insights applicable to advanced aerospace, space-adjacent mobility, and high-reliability thermal control systems of interest to organizations

### Thermal Requirements of Major EV Subsystems

| Subsystem | Recommended Operating Temperature | Potential Consequences of Excessive Temperature |
|------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Li-ion Battery Pack | 15°C – 40°C | Thermal runaway, accelerated aging, capacity loss |
| Electric Motor | 80°C – 120°C (Windings) | Insulation degradation, magnetic performance loss |
| Power Electronics (IGBT/SiC Modules) | < 150°C Junction Temperature | Semiconductor failure, efficiency reduction |
| Cabin HVAC System | 18°C – 26°C | Reduced passenger comfort, increased energy consumption |
